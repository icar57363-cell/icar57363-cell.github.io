🚘 The World of Cars

Cars have changed a lot over the years. Early cars were simple machines, while modern cars use powerful engines, computers, sensors, advanced safety systems, and sometimes electric motors.

Different cars are built for different purposes. Some are made for speed, some for comfort, some for racing, and some for everyday transportation.

⚙️ How Does a Car Work?

🔥 Engine

A traditional petrol engine burns fuel to produce energy. This energy moves pistons, which turn the crankshaft and eventually help turn the wheels.

🛞 Tires

Tires are extremely important because they provide grip between the car and the road. They help the car accelerate, steer, and brake.

🛑 Brakes

Brakes slow the car down by converting its motion into heat. Good brakes are essential for controlling a vehicle safely.

🌬️ Aerodynamics

Aerodynamics is the study of how air moves around objects. Car designers use aerodynamic shapes to reduce air resistance and improve stability.

🧠 Car Quiz

1. What gives a car grip on the road?

A) Windows
B) Tires
C) Headlights

Answer: B) Tires ✅


2. What is the purpose of brakes?

A) Slow the car down
B) Make the car faster
C) Make the engine bigger

Answer: A) Slow the car down ✅


3. What does aerodynamics study?

A) How air moves around objects
B) How fuel is stored
C) How headlights produce light

Answer: A) How air moves around objects ✅
