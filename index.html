🏎️ What Makes a Car Interesting?

Cars are much more than four wheels and an engine. Modern cars combine engineering, computers, aerodynamics, safety systems, and clever designs to get people from one place to another.

⚙️ The Engine

A traditional petrol engine burns fuel and uses the energy produced by combustion to move pistons. Those pistons turn the crankshaft, which eventually helps turn the wheels.

🛞 The Tires

Tires are the only part of a normal car that directly touches the road. They provide grip for accelerating, steering and braking.

🌬️ Aerodynamics

At high speeds, air resistance becomes very important. Car designers shape the body of a car to control the airflow and reduce drag or create extra downforce.

🤯 Crazy Car Facts

🔥 Some performance cars can accelerate from 0 to 100 km/h in only a few seconds.

💻 Modern cars can contain dozens of computers controlling different systems.

🏁 Race cars are designed differently from normal road cars because their main goal is performance.

⚡ Electric cars can deliver their motor's torque very quickly, giving them strong acceleration.

🛑 Brakes convert the car's motion into heat so the vehicle can slow down.
