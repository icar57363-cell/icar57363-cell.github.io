⚙️ The World of Car Engines 🏎️

🔥 What Is an Engine?

A car engine is a machine that converts energy into mechanical motion. In a traditional petrol or diesel car, the engine burns fuel to produce the energy needed to move the vehicle.

🧩 Main Parts of an Engine

  • 🔩 Pistons — move up and down inside the cylinders.
  • ⚙️ Crankshaft — converts piston movement into rotation.
  • 💨 Valves — control the flow of air and exhaust gases.
  • 🔥 Spark plugs — ignite the air-fuel mixture in petrol engines.
  • 🛢️ Oil system — helps lubricate moving engine parts.
  • 🌡️ Cooling system — helps prevent the engine from overheating.

🔄 How Does It Work?

In a typical four-stroke petrol engine, the engine goes through four main stages: intake, compression, power, and exhaust. These stages repeat many times every second while the engine is running.

🏎️ Different Types of Engines

  • ⛽ Petrol engines
  • 🛢️ Diesel engines
  • ⚡ Electric motors
  • 🔋 Hybrid powertrains
  • 🚀 Turbocharged engines

💡 Why Are Engines Important?

Engines are one of the most important parts of a vehicle. They combine mechanical engineering, physics, materials, fuel or electricity, and computer-controlled systems to produce the power needed to move a car.
